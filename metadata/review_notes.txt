Three behaviours are deliberate. A quick pass can misread them.

1. Cold launch can hold a blank dark screen for a few seconds. Wait for the cover or the year plate. That wait is expected.
2. Future days are dimmed and not tappable. Past unmarked days show only a day number and do not respond. Use the outlined "Today" cell ("Open" if unmarked) or footer "Set the tone" to mark today.
3. Tone runs can show "No runs yet." and "Mark today so a neighbor day can join it." Tap "Set the tone" on that state. The list fills after the first mark.
